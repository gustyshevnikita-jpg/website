FAQ

What is an AI agent?

Software that uses a language model to complete multi-step tasks on its own — reading a ticket, looking up an order, drafting and sending a reply — rather than just answering a single prompt.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Setup is a guided form. For anything unusual, we'll help.
